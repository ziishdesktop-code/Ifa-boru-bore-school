Academic Programs

Ifa Boru Bore School provides quality education for students from Grade 9 to Grade 12 following the Ethiopian curriculum.

Grade 9

Mathematics, English, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Geography, History, ICT and Civics.

Grade 10

Advanced study in Mathematics, English, Sciences, Social Sciences and ICT.

Grade 11

Students begin focusing on Natural Science or Social Science streams.

Grade 12

Intensive preparation for the Ethiopian Higher Education Entrance Examination.

Subjects We Offer

📘 Mathematics

Build strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

🧪 Natural Sciences

Physics, Chemistry and Biology with practical learning.

📖 Languages

English, Afan Oromo and Amharic communication skills.

💻 ICT

Computer skills and digital literacy for the modern world.

🌍 Social Sciences

Geography, History, Economics and Civics.

🤖 AI Learning

Students can use our AI Assistant for academic support.
